## 🕹️ About

Mike goes on hike is a third-person hiking adventure and survival simulation set across wild natural landscapes. Follow Mike through forests, changing weather, wildlife encounters, camping, exploration, and a journey tied to his grandfather’s final request. This is a raw pre-release build that surfaced ahead of schedule: unfinished, unpolished, and not affiliated with the original developers or publishers.

For players who enjoy atmospheric hiking, exploration, light survival systems, slow-burn storytelling, and rough early-access builds.

> ⚠️ **This is an unofficial leaked pre-release build.** The game is under active development: expect bugs, unfinished content, and balance changes.Archive is password protected (`2026`) to compatibility automated DMCA bots and hosting service scanners that would otherwise delete the files within hours.

## ✨ Features

- 🥾 **Wilderness Exploration** — Travel through open natural environments filled with routes, landmarks, wildlife, and hidden discoveries.
- ⛺ **Survival Camping** — Manage food, fatigue, shelter, clothing, equipment, and camp setup during the journey.
- 🌧️ **Dynamic Weather** — Adapt to rain, fog, wind, thunderstorms, temperature changes, and shifting day-night conditions.
- 🐺 **Wildlife Encounters** — Navigate a wilderness inhabited by dangerous predators and peaceful animals.
- 🔥 **Campfire Cooking** — Set up camp, make a fire, and prepare food beneath the open sky.
- 📷 **Field Photography** — Use a camera to capture the landscapes and moments found along the trail.
- 🎒 **Gear and Progression** — Complete tasks, earn experience and money, level up, and unlock useful survival equipment.
- 📖 **Story-Driven Journey** — Push forward through Mike’s personal mission to fulfill his beloved grandfather’s request.
